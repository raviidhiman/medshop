🤖 How Automatic Alerts Work
1
App starts → Scheduler starts automatically

Every time your app runs (locally or on Render), the background scheduler starts in a separate thread.

2
Every day at your chosen time

The scheduler wakes up and scans your MongoDB inventory for expired, out-of-stock, low stock, and expiring soon medicines.

3
Sends email only if issues found

If everything is healthy, no email is sent. If problems exist, a detailed alert email is sent to your inbox automatically.

4
One email per day maximum

The scheduler tracks when it last sent and will never send duplicate emails on the same day.

Triggers an email when:

❌Any medicine has quantity = 0 (out of stock)
🚫Any medicine's expiry date has passed
⚠️Any medicine has quantity ≤ 10 (low stock)
⏳Any medicine expires within 30 days
